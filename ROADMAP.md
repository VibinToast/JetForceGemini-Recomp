# Roadmap

What's coming to Jet Force Gemini Recompiled. Plans can change and there are no dates. What has already shipped is in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md), and problems we know about are in [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

## Now: 1.2 (in final testing)

- Smoother, more responsive play: 60 ticks per second for quicker controls, and Frame Interpolation up to your
  monitor's refresh rate.
- Auto Save, a reorganized settings menu and an improved launcher.
- Linux builds (AppImage and portable) and Steam Deck support.
- Many fixes, including crashes in boss fights and texture packs whose names are in capitals.

## Next: 1.3 (planned)

**Mods**
- Mod support.

**Settings menu**
- The game darkens behind the menu while it's open.
- A tab for quitting the game or going back to the launcher.
- B on a controller jumps up to the tabs, so you can switch tabs without scrolling through every option.
- LB / RB move a slider in steps of 10 while the slider is selected.
- A yellow outline on the selected profile, texture pack and similar buttons, so it's easier to see.
- Clearer profile descriptions.
- Touch support on the Steam Deck's screen.
- A better way to use the F10 tools with a controller.

**Gameplay**
- Settings for Floyd's fuel in Floyd missions.

**Visuals**
- In widescreen, the HUD moves out to the corners of the screen.

**Fixes**
- The menu can scroll all the way to the top when a tab has text above its options.
- Shadows in the intro cutscene of the Tribals level.
- The visual glitch on the Floyd screen just before a Floyd mission.
- In the launcher, pressing LB right after RB no longer shows the old page for a moment.

## Planned: online multiplayer

A larger update after 1.3: play the multiplayer modes online with friends, not only on one screen.

## Later (ideas, not scheduled)

- Save and load states.
- Gyro aiming.
- A faster weapon-switching menu.
- An on-screen minimap.
- Other languages.
- Choosing different character models.
- Two-player co-op in the story.
- Other multiplayer improvements.
- A randomizer and a rogue-like mode (likely as mods).
- Full-detail ants in cutscenes when level of detail is turned off.

Have an idea or found a bug? See [Reporting bugs](README.md#reporting-bugs).
