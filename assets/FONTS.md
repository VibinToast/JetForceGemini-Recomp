- `jfg_launcher/JFG-Regular.ttf` and `jfg_launcher/JFG-Bold.ttf` are the JFG font (family "JFG"), and `jfg_launcher/JFG-Black.ttf` its heaviest weight (its own family, "JFG Black"), which the launcher's titles and buttons use. The digits and punctuation and the Bold and Black weights were added for the launcher.
